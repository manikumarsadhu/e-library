Easy Guide How to add your book from Google Drive
  1. 1
    Share on Drive

    Upload your PDF in Google Drive, click Share, set General access to “Anyone with the link” (Viewer), and copy the link.

  2. 2
    Fill Details & Paste Link

    Enter Title, Author, and paste the copied link in Google Drive Link.

  3. 3
    Choose Visibility & Passcode

    Pick Public or Private, and set a 4+ char passcode (used to unlock private books or delete later).

  4. 4
    Click Add Book

    Your page-1 cover loads automatically and you can start reading right away.
